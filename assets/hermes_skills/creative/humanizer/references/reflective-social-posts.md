# Reflective social posts from event notes

Use this reference when the user wants a心得文/LinkedIn post/personal reflection that can be repurposed across platforms.

## Goal
Turn raw impressions into a post that sounds like a real person reflecting on what they learned, not a polished marketing blurb.

## Reliable structure
1. Opening: one sentence of genuine reaction.
2. What impressed you: 1-2 concrete observations from the talk or work.
3. Why it mattered to you: connect to your own work habits, doubts, or blind spots.
4. The takeaway: one clear idea you want readers to remember.
5. Close: brief forward-looking line that feels personal, not corporate.

## Voice cues that fit this class of writing
- Use first person naturally: "我最大的感受是", "這讓我重新思考".
- Keep the language warm and specific.
- Prefer one clear thesis over many parallel points.
- Mix admiration with a small amount of self-reflection or tension.
- Mention the concrete thing the speaker did, then the broader lesson.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Turning it into a press release.
- Using too many abstract nouns like "價值", "趨勢", "洞察" without grounding them in one concrete example.
- Over-formatting with bullets unless the platform calls for it.
- Ending with a generic positive conclusion.

## Useful angles from this session
- Data visualization can be playful and still rigorous.
- Many good ideas stay in our heads instead of becoming working prototypes.
- A data analyst can use generative AI to bridge back end, front end, and interaction design.
- "放權" to agents can be framed as exploration: let the model go into the parts we don't yet understand and see what it finds.

## Example thesis pattern
"我本來以為 X 只是展示技巧，結果我更在意的是 Y：它提醒我，現在我們可以把一部分未知交給 agent，讓自己有更多空間去探索真正重要的問題。"
