# Report Challenge Checklist

Use selectively. Do not mechanically list every item.

## Business Driver

- What product or service actually drives earnings?
- What changed recently?
- Is demand structural, cyclical, or temporary?
- Is growth from volume, price, mix, utilization, or market share?

## Evidence Strength

- Is the growth driver already visible in revenue?
- Is the project qualified, sampled, won, ordered, shipped, or mass-produced?
- Is revenue contribution disclosed?
- Is EPS contribution disclosed?
- Is the report relying on vague phrases such as “potential opportunity” or “long-term benefit”?

## Earnings Quality

- Is EPS growth recurring?
- Are there disposal gains, FX gains, mark-to-market gains, or accounting reclassifications?
- Is margin improvement structural?
- Are CAPEX and depreciation aligned?
- Is working capital deteriorating?

## Valuation

- Which year EPS is used?
- Is that year too distant?
- Is EPS normalized?
- Is the multiple historically demanding?
- Has rerating already occurred?
- Does the valuation require an unproven optionality to become real?

## Industry Structure

- Is supply expanding?
- Are bottlenecks temporary or durable?
- Is the company dependent on one customer?
- Does technology substitution create risk?
- Is a short-term shock being misread as a long-term trend?

## Final Output

A good answer should state:

- base case;
- upside case;
- fragile assumption;
- next evidence to monitor.
